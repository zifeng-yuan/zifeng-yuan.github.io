I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Professor Benjamin Tee. Prior to that, I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at NUS, advised by Professor Aaron Danner.

My research lies at the intersection of AI and photonics, with a focus on photonic AI infrastructure. I build it from the device up: polarization-engineered VCSELs serve as fast and drive-efficient binary nodes, and on top of them I develop photonic computing hardware ranging from Ising machines to integrated matrix engines for neural networks. In parallel I study how vision-language models and large language models reason about physical and scientific problems.
